Walsh spectra of all known APN functions over GF(2^8)¶

Content extracted from data/Walsh_spectra_of_all_known_APN_functions_over_GF(2%5E8).txt. Original page:

https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/Walsh_spectra_of_all_known_APN_functions_over_GF(2%5E8)

Overview¶

The tables below contain the Walsh spectra for all known instances of APN functions over GF(2^8). All of these \(8192\) functions have one of the following three Walsh spectra.

Gold-Like Spectrum¶

This is the same Walsh spectrum as the Gold functions:

\(\{-32^{2380}, -16^{20400}, 0^{16320}, 16^{23120}, 32^{3060}\}\).

Walsh Spectrum of Type 1¶

\(\{-64^{6}, -32^{2240}, -16^{20880}, 0^{15600}, 16^{23664}, 32^{2880}, 64^{10}\}\).

Walsh Spectrum of Type 2¶

\(\{-64^{12}, -32^{2100}, -16^{21360}, 0^{14880}, 16^{24208}, 32^{2700}, 64^{20}\}\).

There are \(12\) functions with a Walsh spectrum of type 2, \(487\) functions with a Walsh spectrum of type 1, and \(7693\) functions with a Gold-like Walsh spectrum.
