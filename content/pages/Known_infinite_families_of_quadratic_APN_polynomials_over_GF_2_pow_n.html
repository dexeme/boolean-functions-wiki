Known infinite families of quadratic APN polynomials over GF(2^n)¶

Content partially extracted from data/Known_infinite_families_of_quadratic_APN_polynomials_over_GF(2%5En).txt. Original page:

\(N^\circ\) Functions Conditions References
C1-C2 \(x^{2^s+1} + u^{2^k-1}x^{2^{ik}+2^{mk+s}}\) \(n = pk\)
\(\gcd(k, 3) = \gcd(s, 3k) = 1\)
\(p \in \{3\), \(4\}\)
\(i = sk \pmod p\)
\(m = p - i\)
\(n \geq 12\)
\(u\) primitive in \(\mathbb{F}_{2^n}^*\)		 [BCL08]
C3 \(sx^{q+1} + x^{2^i+1} + x^{q(2^i+1)} + cx^{2^iq+1} + c^qx^{2^i+q}\) \(q = 2^m\)
\(n = 2m\)
\(\gcd(i, m) = 1\)
\(c \in \mathbb{F}_{2^n}\)
\(s \in \mathbb{F}_{2^n} \setminus \mathbb{F}_q\)
\(X^{2^i+1} + cX^{2^i} + c^qX + 1\) has no solution \(x\) s.t. \(x^{q+1} = 1\)		 [BC08]
C4 \(x^3 + a^{-1}\text{Tr}_n(a^3x^9)\) \(a \neq 0\) [BCL09a]
C5 \(x^3 + a^{-1}\text{Tr}_n^3(a^3x^9 + a^6x^{18})\) \(3|n\)
\(a \neq 0\)		 [BCL09b]
C6 \(x^3 + a^{-1}\text{Tr}_n^3(a^6x^{18} + a^{12}x^{36})\) \(3|n\)
\(a \neq 0\)		 [BCL09b]
C7-C9 \(ux^{2^s+1} + u^{2^k}x^{2^{-k}+2^{k+s}} + vx^{2^{-k}+1} + wu^{2^k+1}x^{2^s+2^{k+s}}\) \(n = 3k\)
\(\gcd(k, 3) = \gcd(s, 3k) = 1\)
\(v\), \(w \in \mathbb{F}_{2^k}\)
\(vw \neq 1\)
\(3|(k+s)\)
\(u\) primitive in \(\mathbb{F}_{2^n}^*\)		 [BBMM11]
C10 \((x+x^{2^m})^{2^k+1} + u'(ux+u^{2^m}x^{2^m})^{(2^k+1)2^i} + u(x+x^{2^m})(ux+u^{2^m}x^{2^m})\) \(n=2m\)
\(m \geq 2\) even
\(\gcd(k,m)=1\) and \(i \geq 2\) even
\(u\) primitive in \(\mathbb{F}_{2^n}^*\)
\(u' \in \mathbb{F}_{2^m}\) not a cube		 [ZP13]
C11 \(L(x)^{2^i}x + L(x)x^{2^i}\) \(n=km\)
\(m > 1\)
\(\gcd(n,i)=1\)
\(L(x) = \sum_{j=0}^{k-1} a_j x^{2^{jm}}\) satisfies conditions in Theorem 6.3 of [7]		 [BCC+20]
C12 \(ut(x)(x^q+x) + t(x)^{2^{2i}+2^{3i}} + at(x)^{2^{2i}}(x^q+x)^{2^i} + b(x^q+x)^{2^i+1}\) \(n=2m\)
\(q=2^m\)
\(\gcd(m,i)=1\)
\(t(x)=u^qx+x^qu\)
\(X^{2^i+1}+aX+b\) has no solution over \(\mathbb{F}_{2^m}\)		 [Tan19]
C13 \(x^3 + a(x^{2^i+1})^{2^k} + bx^{3 \cdot 2^m} + c(x^{2^{i+m}+2^m})^{2^k}\) \(n=2m=10\)
\((a,b,c)=(\beta, 0, 0)\)
\(i=3\)
\(k=2\)
\(\beta\) primitive in \(\mathbb{F}_{2^2}\)
\(n=2m\)
\(m\) odd
\(3 \nmid m\)
\((a,b,c)=(\beta, \beta^2, 1)\)
\(\beta\) primitive in \(\mathbb{F}_{2^2}\)
\(i \in \{m-2\), \(m\), \(2m-1\), \((m-2)^{-1} \pmod n\}\)		 [ZP13]
