APN polynomials over GF(2^n) CCZ-inequivalent to quadratic functions and monomials¶

Content partially extracted from data/APN_polynomials_over_GF(2%5En)_CCZ-inequivalent_to_quadratic_functions_and_monomials.txt. Original page: https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/APN_polynomials_over_GF(2%5En)_CCZ-inequivalent_to_quadratic_functions_and_monomials

O polinômio

\(x^3 + \alpha^{17}(x^{17} + x^{18} + x^{20} + x^{24}) + \alpha^{14}( \text{Tr}(\alpha^{52}x^3 + \alpha^6x^5 + \alpha^{19}x^7 + \alpha^{28}x^{11} + \alpha^2x^{13}) + (\alpha^2x)^9 + (\alpha^2x)^{18} + (\alpha^2x)^{36} + x^{21} + x^{42})\)

where \(\alpha\) is a primitive in \(GF(2^6)\), is the only known example of an APN function CCZ-inequivalent to a monomial or quadratic function.

A Magma implementation of the polynomial is available.
