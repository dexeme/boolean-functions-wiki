Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimensions up to 10¶

Content extracted from data/Sigma_multiplicities_for_APN_functions_in_dimensions_up_to_10.txt. Original page:

https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/Sigma_multiplicities_for_APN_functions_in_dimensions_up_to_10

For all known APN functions over \(GF(2^n)\) with \(n\) up to \(10\), the following tables list the partitions of \(GF(2^n)\) induced by the multiplicities of the Sigma sets \(\Sigma_F^k(0)\) for \(k=4\), as explained in the SETA paper on testing EA-equivalences.

Due to the large number of functions, the results for dimension 8 are listed on Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimension 8.

Dimension 7¶

In dimension 7, all tested functions induce the trivial partition of the field into zero and non-zero elements. This includes the inverse APN function.

Dimension 8¶

The results for dimension 8 are given on Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimension 8 due to the large number of functions and distinct partitions.

Dimension 9¶

In dimension 9, all tested functions, including the inverse function, from the list of CCZ-inequivalent representatives from the known APN families for dimensions up to 11, as well as those obtained via self-equivalences, have the same trivial partition into zero and non-zero elements.
