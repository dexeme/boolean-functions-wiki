Overview¶

For all known APN functions over \(GF(2^8)\), the following tables list the partitions of \(GF(2^8)\) induced by the multiplicities of the Sigma sets \(\Sigma_F^k(0)\) for \(k=4\), as explained in the SETA paper on testing EA-equivalences.
