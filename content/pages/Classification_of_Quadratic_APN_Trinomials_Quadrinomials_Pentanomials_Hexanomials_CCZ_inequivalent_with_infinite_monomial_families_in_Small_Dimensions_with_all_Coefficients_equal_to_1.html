Overview¶

The following tables list \(CCZ\)-inequivalent representatives found by systematically searching for APN functions among all trinomials, quadrinomials, pentanomials and hexanomials with coefficients in \(\mathbb{F}_{2}\) over \(\mathbb{F}_{2^n}\) with \(6 \le n \le 11\). The tables also list which equivalence class from [EP09] the functions belong to. Only polynomials inequivalent to power functions are considered. If the polynomial is equivalent to a family from the table of infinite families, this is also listed.
