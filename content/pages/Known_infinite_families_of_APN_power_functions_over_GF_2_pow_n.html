Known infinite families of APN power functions over GF(2^n)¶

Overview¶

The following table provides a summary of all known infinite families of power APN functions of the form \(F(x) = x^d\).

Family Exponent Conditions \(deg(x^d)\) Reference
Gold \(2^i + 1\) \(gcd(i, n) = 1\) \(2\) [Gol68], [Nyb94]
Kasami \(2^{2i} - 2^i + 1\) \(gcd(i, n) = 1\) \(i + 1\) [JW93], [Kas71]
Welch \(2^t + 3\) \(n = 2t + 1\) \(3\) [Dob99b]
Niho \(2^t + 2^{t/2} - 1\), \(t\) even
\(2^t + 2^{(3t+1)/2} - 1\), \(t\) odd		 \(n = 2t + 1\) \((t+2)/2\)
\(t + 1\)		 [Dob99a]
Inverse \(2^{2t} - 1\) \(n = 2t + 1\) \(n - 1\) [Nyb94], [BD94]
Dobbertin \(2^{4i} + 2^{3i} + 2^{2i} + 2^i - 1\) \(n = 5i\) \(i + 3\) [Dob01]

[BD94, Dob99a, Dob99b, Dob01, Gol68, JW93, Kas71, Nyb94]
