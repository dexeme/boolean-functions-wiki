Some APN functions CCZ-equivalent to Gold functions and EA-inequivalent to power functions over GF(2^n)¶

https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/Some_APN_functions_CCZ-equivalent_to_Gold_functions_and_EA-inequivalent_to_power_functions_over_GF(2%5En)

The following table lists APN functions that are CCZ-equivalent to Gold functions and EA-inequivalent to power functions over \(\mathbb{F}_{2^n}\) [BCP05].

Function Conditions \(d^{\circ}\)
\(\displaystyle x^{2^i+1}+(x^{2^i}+x+\operatorname{Tr}_n(1)+1)\operatorname{Tr}_n(x^{2^i+1}+x\operatorname{Tr}_n(1))\) | \(n\geqslant 4\) | \(\gcd(i,n)=1\) \(3\)
\(\displaystyle \left[x+\operatorname{Tr}_n^3(x^{2(2^i+1)}+x^{4(2^i+1)})+\operatorname{Tr}_n(x)\operatorname{Tr}_n^3(x^{2^i+1}+x^{2^{2i}(2^i+1)})\right]^{2^i+1}\) | \(6|n\) | \(\gcd(i,n)=1\) \(4\)
| \(\displaystyle x^{2^i+1}+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x^{2^i+1})+x^{2^i}\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)+x\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)^{2^i}\) | \(+\left[\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)^{2^i+1}+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x^{2^i+1})+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)\right]^{\frac{1}{2^i+1}}\left(x^{2^i}+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)^{2^i}+1\right)\) | \(+\left[\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)^{2^i+1}+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x^{2^i+1})+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)\right]^{\frac{2^i}{2^i+1}}\left(x+\operatorname{Tr}_n^m(x)\right)\) | \(m\neq n\) | \(n\) odd | \(m|n\) | \(\gcd(i,n)=1\) \(m+2\)

References

[BCP05]

Lilya Budaghyan, Claude Carlet, and Alexander Pott. New Classes of Almost Bent and Almost Perfect Nonlinear Polynomials. June 2005. arXiv:math/0506604. doi:10.48550/arXiv.math/0506604.
