Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimensions up to 10
=============================================================

Content extracted from ``data/Sigma_multiplicities_for_APN_functions_in_dimensions_up_to_10.txt``.
Original page:

:link:`https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/Sigma_multiplicities_for_APN_functions_in_dimensions_up_to_10  <https://boolean.wiki.uib.no/Sigma_multiplicities_for_APN_functions_in_dimensions_up_to_10>`

For all known APN functions over `GF(2^n)` with `n` up to `10`, the following
tables list the partitions of `GF(2^n)` induced by the multiplicities of the
Sigma sets `\Sigma_F^k(0)` for `k=4`, as explained in the SETA paper on
testing EA-equivalences.

Due to the large number of functions, the results for dimension 8 are listed on
:ref:`Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimension 8 <sigma_multiplicities_for_apn_functions_in_dimension_8>`.

Dimension 6
-----------

The observed sizes of the partition for the known switching classes are given
in the following table. The functions are indexed according to
:ref:`Known switching classes of APN functions over GF(2^n) for n = 5,6,7,8 <known_switching_classes_of_apn_functions_over_gf_2_pow_n_for_n_eq_5_6_7_8>`.

Dimension 7
-----------

In dimension 7, all tested functions induce the trivial partition of the field
into zero and non-zero elements. This includes the inverse APN function.

Dimension 8
-----------

The results for dimension 8 are given on
:ref:`Sigma multiplicities for APN functions in dimension 8 <sigma_multiplicities_for_apn_functions_in_dimension_8>`
due to the large number of functions and distinct partitions.

Dimension 9
-----------

In dimension 9, all tested functions, including the inverse function, from the
list of
:ref:`CCZ-inequivalent representatives from the known APN families for dimensions up to 11 <ccz_inequivalent_apn_functions_from_the_known_apn_classes_over_gf_2_pow_n_for_n_between_6_and_11>`,
as well as those obtained via self-equivalences, have the same trivial
partition into zero and non-zero elements.

Dimension 10
------------

Functions 1-10 are indexed according to
:ref:`CCZ-inequivalent representatives from the known APN families for dimensions up to 11 <ccz_inequivalent_apn_functions_from_the_known_apn_classes_over_gf_2_pow_n_for_n_between_6_and_11>`.
The remaining functions correspond to the ones found via self-equivalences and
are indexed in the same way as in the provided dataset.
