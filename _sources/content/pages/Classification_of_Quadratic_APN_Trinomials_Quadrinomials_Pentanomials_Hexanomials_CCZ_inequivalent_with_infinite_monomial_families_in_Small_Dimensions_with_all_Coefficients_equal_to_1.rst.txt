Overview
--------
The following tables list `CCZ`-inequivalent representatives found by systematically searching for
APN functions among all trinomials, quadrinomials, pentanomials and hexanomials with coefficients
in `\mathbb{F}_{2}` over `\mathbb{F}_{2^n}` with `6 \le n \le 11`. The tables also list which equivalence class
from :cite:p:`Edel:200900` the functions belong to. Only polynomials inequivalent to
power functions are considered.
If the polynomial is equivalent to a family from the :ref:`table of infinite families <know_infinite_families_of_quadratic_APN_polynomials_over_GF_2_pow_n>`, this is also listed.
