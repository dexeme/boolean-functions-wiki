Overview
--------

This page lists the CCZ-equivalence status of APN polynomial families by dimension from `n=6` to `n=11`.
The following table presents CCZ-inequivalent representatives from all CCZ-equivalence classes corresponding to the
:ref:`Known infinite families of APN power functions <know_infinite_families_of_APN_power_functions_over_GF_2_pow_n>`
over `GF(2^n)` and the :ref:`Known infinite families of quadratic APN polynomials <know_infinite_families_of_quadratic_APN_polynomials_over_GF_2_pow_n>`
over `GF(2^n)` for `n` from `6` to `11`.
Values of various invariants are given in the cases when this is possible; empty cells in the invariant columns indicate
that their computation is infeasible using our current resources.
