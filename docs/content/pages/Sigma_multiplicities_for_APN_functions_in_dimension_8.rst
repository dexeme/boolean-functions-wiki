Overview
--------

For all known APN functions over `GF(2^8)`, the following tables list the
partitions of `GF(2^8)` induced by the multiplicities of the Sigma sets
`\Sigma_F^k(0)` for `k=4`, as explained in the SETA paper on testing
EA-equivalences.

Previously Known APN Functions
------------------------------

The first table lists the sizes of the partitions for the previously known
8180 functions, including those obtained by the matrix method. The indexing is
the same as in
:ref:`Known instances of APN functions over GF(2^8) <known_instances_of_apn_functions_over_gf_2_pow_8>`.
